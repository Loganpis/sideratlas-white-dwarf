NO.003 — THE WHITE DWARF
@sideratlas
DA / ZZ Ceti — 0.593 M☉ — 11,290 K

Warm and cool regions brighten out of step
Six gravity modes interfere across the surface
Hydrogen shapes the spectrum and darkens the limb
Gravity slightly redshifts escaping light

CALCULATED
Six adiabatic interior g-mode frequencies from a WDEC structure with GYRE
Dipole and quadrupole surface patterns
Periods ≈ 247, 287, 316, 349, 397 and 488 s
R ≈ 9,018 km; T_eff = T₀(1 + δF/F)^(1/4)
Local DA spectral radiance and limb darkening
Weak light bending, redshift and relative photon delays
z ≈ 0.000097; physical light curve for a fixed observer

ASSUMPTIONS
Representative star, not a reconstruction of a named object
Mode subset, m values, phases and surface amplitudes are prescribed
No validated convective driving or nonlinear saturation
Hybrid 1D LTE hydrogen spectra and 3D broadband limb fits
Mean atmospheric convection effects are retained
The local granulation experiment failed validation and is excluded
Realistic cells are unresolved here; no enlarged cells or invented flicker
Nonmagnetic spherical star; no spots, rotation or imposed deformation
Blue-to-white colors map temperature departures, not true visible color
Fixed ±160 K scale; asinh softening 12 K
Extra display gain exp(1.8u), with u the normalized asinh temperature
Fixed exposure and tone mapping; physical amplitudes are unchanged
Normalized virtual optical PSF; 4.5% redistributed into broad wings
The halo adds no stellar emission
Camera scan is independent of the fixed-observer light curve
32 seconds span 1,721.6 physical seconds at 53.8× speed
Continuous evolution without forced repetition
