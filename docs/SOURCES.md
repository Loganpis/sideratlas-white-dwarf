# Scientific sources and exact roles

Only sources that supply or explain a component of the final model are listed. Citation does not imply their authors endorse this visualization.

1. **Nauenberg, M. (1972), “Analytic Approximations to the Mass-Radius Relation and Energy of Zero-Temperature Stars,” ApJ 175, 417.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/151568). Supplies the cold degenerate mass–radius approximation. Finite-temperature stellar structure is not solved.
2. **Koester, D. (2010), “White dwarf spectra and atmosphere models,” Memorie della Società Astronomica Italiana 81, 921.** [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.0482). Atmosphere framework. Actual pure-H DA fluxes come from the [SVO Koester model service](https://svo2.cab.inta-csic.es/theory/newov2/index.php?models=koester2). `data/spectra-provenance.json` records each exact public download URL, numerical grid point and SHA-256. SVO states that the models are published with the author’s permission. SVO describes air wavelengths, emergent 4π Eddington flux, LTE and hydrostatic plane-parallel pure-H atmospheres. We do not assert an unspecified mixing-length parameter or that these 1D models are identical to the Claret 3D models.
3. **Tremblay, P.-E. & Bergeron, P. (2009), “Spectroscopic Analysis of DA White Dwarfs: Stark Broadening of Hydrogen Lines Including Nonideal Effects,” ApJ 696, 1755.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/696/2/1755). The SVO service attributes updated hydrogen broadening to this work and a 2015 private communication from Tremblay. No separate unpublished dataset is claimed to have been obtained here.
4. **Claret, A., Cukanovaite, E., Burdge, K. B., Tremblay, P.-E., Parsons, S. G. & Marsh, T. R. (2020), “Doppler beaming factors for white dwarfs, main sequence stars, and giant stars: Limb-darkening coefficients for 3D (DA and DB) white dwarf models,” A&A 641, A157.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202038436), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.15715), [author tables at CDS](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/J/A+A/641/A157/). Uses only DA-3D UBVRI four-term limb profiles from `tablea4.dat`. No Doppler beaming factor or gravity-darkening calculation is used. Original table and ReadMe retained as `data/claret-2020-a4.dat` and `data/claret-2020-ReadMe.txt`. The hybrid wavelength reconstruction is our stated approximation.
5. **Romero, A. D. et al. (2012), “Toward ensemble asteroseismology of ZZ Ceti stars with fully evolutionary models,” MNRAS 420, 1462–1480.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2966.2011.20134.x), [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1109.6682), [institutional record and paper](https://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/handle/10915/84684). Provides examples of observed ZZ Ceti periods, including G117-B15A's 215.20, 270.46 and 304.05 s clocks. This visualization does not use the paper's measured amplitudes as local amplitudes or adopt its seismic solution; its chosen spherical harmonics are not inferred from that star.
6. **Wu, Y. & Goldreich, P. (1999), “Gravity Modes in ZZ Ceti Stars. II. Eigenvalues and Eigenfunctions,” ApJ 519, 783.** [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/307412), [author institutional copy](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/vncm5-dak37). Physical context for nonradial g-mode flux variations and the importance of the nonadiabatic convection response. Their interior eigenfunctions and driving calculation are not reproduced; our prescribed surface flux modes are a more limited approximation.
7. **CIE (2019), “Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer.”** [Official dataset](https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b). Actual unmodified 360–830 nm table and metadata used for XYZ integration. CC BY-SA 4.0; see `data/PROVENANCE.md`.
8. **W3C, CSS Color Module Level 4, color conversion sample code.** [Primary specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#color-conversion-code). Standard XYZ-to-linear-sRGB matrix and sRGB transfer conventions. Observer spectra are not white-balanced to the star.

Direct Schwarzschild deflection, redshift and differential-time formulas are implemented as the explicit geodesic integrals in `MODEL.md`, with independently evaluated numerical tests. No black-hole or pulsar emission prescription is reused as white-dwarf physics. Latin Modern font attribution and licenses are in `fonts/`; the previous SiderAtlas export pipeline is extended with high-depth input and final quantization error diffusion.

## Data-use distinction

These are numerical atmosphere/limb/color tables, not borrowed animation frames or full research-paper reproductions. The CIE license and original metadata are retained verbatim. Other model inputs retain their authorship, original tables and service provenance; this project does not relicense those inputs under the software's MIT license. Source-specific notices, scientific citations, exact download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are retained. SVO states that its Koester models are published with the author's permission. Attribution and public data availability do not imply third-party endorsement.
