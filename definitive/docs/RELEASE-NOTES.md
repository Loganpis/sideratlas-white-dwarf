# v2.0.0 — six interior modes and luminous imaging

The final film now uses six solved GYRE gravity-mode frequencies from the
retained WDEC equilibrium, replacing v1's three prescribed surface-mode
clocks. The adopted structure is 0.593 solar masses, 9,018 km and 11,290 K.
Surface excitation, phases and flux normalization remain explicit assumptions.

One continuous 32-second scan preserves version B's cinematic camera and
minimal composition. Spectral DA radiance, limb darkening, weak relativistic
propagation and a normalized virtual PSF produce the luminous image. The
six-percent scattering wing redistributes existing light. Surface colors
remain a fixed enhanced temperature diagnostic.

The surface-physics investigation uses published radiation-hydrodynamic
statistics to evaluate granulation resolution. Individual granules are much
smaller than a pixel and are not enlarged. No magnetic spots or unvalidated
convection response are imposed on unchanged nonmagnetic modes.

The package includes the physical fixed-observer light curve, exact input
checksums, saved eigenproblem results, current numerical/media verification,
source-frame references and caption. It distributes one final movie and no
alternative or comparison Reels. The v1 tag preserves the earlier release.
