NO.003 — THE WHITE DWARF
@sideratlas
DA / ZZ Ceti — 0.593 M☉ — 11,290 K

Warm and cool regions brighten out of step
Six gravity modes interfere across the surface
Hydrogen shapes the spectrum and darkens the limb
Gravity slightly redshifts the escaping light

CALCULATED
Six interior g-mode eigenfrequencies from a WDEC structure with GYRE
Dipole and quadrupole surface patterns
Periods ≈ 247, 287, 316, 349, 397 and 488 s
R ≈ 9,018 km; T_eff = T₀(1 + δF/F)^(1/4)
DA spectral radiance at each local temperature
Limb darkening, weak light bending, redshift and relative photon delays
z ≈ 0.000097; a separate physical light curve for a fixed observer

ASSUMPTIONS
Representative star, not a reconstruction of a named object
Excited subset, m values, phases and surface amplitudes are prescribed
No validated convective driving or nonlinear saturation calculation
Hybrid 1D LTE hydrogen spectra and 3D broadband limb fits
Published RHD granules are about 0.85 km across, only 0.05 pixel here
Individual granules remain unresolved; no enlarged cells or invented turbulence
Nonmagnetic spherical star; no imposed magnetic spots, rotation or deformation
Blue and warm tones show temperature departures, not visible stellar colors
Fixed ±160 K scale; asinh contrast with 18 K softening
Contrast changes the display, never the temperature amplitudes
Subtle spectral halo from a normalized virtual optical PSF
Six percent of the light is redistributed into broad wings, not added emission
Camera motion changes the view, not the fixed-observer light curve
32 seconds span 1,721.6 physical seconds at 53.8× speed
Continuous evolution, with no forced seamless repetition
