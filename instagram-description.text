NO.003 — THE WHITE DWARF
@sideratlas
ZZ Ceti · hydrogen atmosphere

Six gravity modes overlap across the surface
Hot and cool regions evolve as their phases interfere
Magnified cellular granulation reveals a convection approximation
An optical halo makes the star appear luminous

CALCULATED
Representative WDEC structure: 0.593 M☉, radius 9018 km, Teff 11290 K
Six adiabatic GYRE modes, ℓ = 1–2; observed periods 247–488 s
Surface flux harmonics; Teff = T₀(1 + δF/F)¼
DA spectra, limb darkening, weak gravitational bending, delays and redshift z ≈ 0.000097
Separate light curve uses a fixed observer

ASSUMPTIONS
Mode selection, phases and amplitudes prescribed; not predicted excitation
Static 1D LTE spectra plus 3D-based broadband limb fits
Cellular convection is a statistical approximation, not validated RHD
DA neighbor models suggest 0.85 km cells and 17.3% bolometric intensity RMS; empirical decay ≈ 0.11 s
Weighted Voronoi morphology, growth and drift prescribed; no fluid velocity solved
Grey intensity proxy; not calculated optical cell spectra or local cell temperatures
Cells enlarged ≈551×; global mean normalized; cellular display contrast exponent 4
Mode evolution accelerated 53.8×; convection slowed 20× on a separate display clock
These clocks do not represent simultaneous physical evolution
No calculated nonlinear mode–convection coupling, rotation, deformation or magnetic spots
96% false-color blending shows mode ΔTeff within ±160 K; asinh softening 4 K, log-luminance gain 2.7
Prescribed Airy+jitter+Moffat optics; 12% broad scatter, halo display gain 6, fixed exposure 1.7
At 1080 width: Airy zero 3.8 px at 550 nm; jitter 0.6 px; Moffat α=85 px, β=2.5
Glow and contrast are imaging enhancements, not extra emission
32 seconds; slow moving camera; no forced periodic loop
