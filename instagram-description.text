NO.003 — THE WHITE DWARF
@sideratlas
DA / ZZ Ceti — 0.6 M☉ — 12,000 K

Warm and cool regions brighten and fade out of step
Nonradial modes interfere across the surface
Their combined temperature pattern continually changes
Hydrogen shapes the spectrum and darkens the limb
Gravity slightly redshifts the light

CALCULATED
Cold mass–radius estimate: R ≈ 8,658 km
Three spherical-harmonic photospheric flux patterns
T_eff = T₀(1 + δF/F)^(1/4)
Periods: 215.20, 270.46 and 304.05 s
Interpolated DA atmosphere spectra and flux-normalized limb emission
Atmospheric radiance evaluated at the varying local temperature
Schwarzschild light bending, redshift and differential photon delays
z ≈ 0.000102, equivalent to ≈30.7 km/s
Separate physical light curve for a fixed observer at i = 55°, az = 0°

ASSUMPTIONS AND VISUALIZATION
Representative star, not a reconstruction of a named white dwarf
Surface mode geometry, phases and local amplitudes are prescribed
Local bolometric semi-amplitudes: 3%, 1.2%, 0.8%
No interior eigenmode, convective driving or fluid-motion solution
Spherical surface; rotation, displacement and Doppler velocities omitted
Hybrid 1D hydrogen spectra plus 3D broadband limb fits
Finite-temperature radius corrections and pulsational gravity changes omitted
Blue and red are false colors of temperature departures, not visible emitted colors
Fixed −160 to +160 K scale; asinh contrast with 18 K softening
Contrast enhancement changes only the display, not physical amplitudes
No automatic rescaling, invented textures or exaggerated deformation
One slow camera scan; it does not rotate the star or change the fixed-observer light curve
32 seconds cover 1,721.6 physical seconds, sped up 53.8×
Continuous evolution, not four repeated clips or a forced seamless loop
